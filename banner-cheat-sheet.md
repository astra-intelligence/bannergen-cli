## Key Safe-Area Rules

- **YouTube Banner**: Text/logos must stay within 1546×423 px safe area (centered)
- **Twitter Header**: 1500×500, but text stays within ~1200×400 for mobile
- **Facebook Cover**: Text limited to 20% of image area
- **LinkedIn Banner**: Full bleed, no overlay text required
